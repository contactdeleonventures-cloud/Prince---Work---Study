# Daily Routine: v1

> Built around: work 6 AM–3 PM daily, class 5–7:30 PM, gym Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun, ~7.5 hrs sleep.
> Assumes **no class on weekends** and short commutes. Adjust once travel times are confirmed.

---

## Every Day: Morning (fixed)

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 4:45 AM | Wake up, drink water, make bed |
| 4:50 | **Prayer** (5–10 min) |
| 5:00 | Shower, dress, breakfast |
| 5:20 | Commute. **No social media.** Plan the day in your head |
| 5:45 | Arrive, prep station |
| 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM | **Shift at Dr. Coffee** |

### Built into the shift (no extra time needed)
- **Latte art:** 3 intentional pours, each one judged honestly
- **Speaking reps:** 1 real conversation with a customer or co-worker beyond the order
- **Content capture:** 1–2 clips of bar life (if allowed) for the barista pillar
- **Break:** read 10 pages, *then* phone

---
