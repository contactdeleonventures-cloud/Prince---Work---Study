## The Split (per ₱6,000 cutoff)

| Bucket | Per cutoff | Per month | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Needs** | ₱3,000 | ₱6,000 | Food, transport, load/data, school stuff, family contribution |
| **Emergency fund** | ₱1,200 | ₱2,400 | Untouchable. Only for real emergencies |
| **Growth** | ₱700 | ₱1,400 | Books, gym, editing tools, brand gear, law prep |
| **Pejay fund** | ₱600 | ₱1,200 | QR menu upgrade costs (hosting, domain, printing, tools) |
| **Wants** | ₱500 | ₱1,000 | Fun money. This is the ₱1k/month budget |
| **Total** | **₱6,000** | **₱12,000** | |

## Rules

1. **Pay yourself first.** On payday, move the Emergency + Pejay money to a separate savings account *before* spending anything.
2. **Wants cap = ₱500 per cutoff.** When it's gone, it's gone. No borrowing from Needs or savings.
3. **Track every peso for 30 days.** You can't optimize what you don't measure.
4. **Emergency fund target: ₱12,000** (about 2 months of Needs). At ₱2,400/month, you hit it in **5 months**.
5. Once the emergency fund is full, redirect that ₱1,200/cutoff into **Pejay** and **investing**.

## Payday Checklist (every 15th and end of month)

- [ ] Transfer ₱1,200 → Emergency savings
- [ ] Transfer ₱600 → Pejay fund
- [ ] Set aside ₱3,000 for Needs
- [ ] Log last cutoff's spending: did you stay under?
